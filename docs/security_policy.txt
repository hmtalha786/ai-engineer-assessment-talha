Information Security Policy

SECRETS AND CREDENTIALS

Secrets are never committed to version control. They live in the secrets manager and are injected
as environment variables at runtime. A secret that reaches a repository is treated as compromised
even if the commit is deleted: it must be rotated within four hours and the exposure recorded in
the security log.

Access tokens issued to services expire after ninety days. Personal access tokens expire after
thirty. Rotation is the owning team's responsibility, and an expiring credential raises a warning
fourteen days before it lapses.

Shared accounts are not permitted. Where a vendor supports only one login, the credential is held
in the secrets manager and access is granted individually and logged.

ACCESS CONTROL

Access follows least privilege. An engineer receives the permissions their role requires and no
more. Production database write access is granted for a named task and expires after twenty-four
hours; standing write access is held only by the platform team.

Access reviews run quarterly. Each manager confirms that everyone on their team still needs the
access they hold. Access is revoked within one business day of an employee changing role, and
within one hour of them leaving the company.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every system that supports it. Hardware keys are
required for production infrastructure and for the secrets manager; app-based codes are
acceptable elsewhere. SMS is not accepted as a second factor.

HANDLING CUSTOMER DATA

Customer data is classified as confidential. It is not copied to a laptop, pasted into a
third-party tool, or used in a test environment. Where realistic test data is needed, it is
generated or anonymised, never sampled from production.

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Backups are encrypted with a separate key and restore
procedures are tested twice a year. Personal data is retained only as long as the stated purpose
requires, and deletion requests are completed within thirty days.

VULNERABILITY RESPONSE

Reported vulnerabilities are triaged within one business day. Critical vulnerabilities are fixed
or mitigated within seven days, high within thirty, and medium within ninety. A vulnerability that
is being actively exploited is handled as a priority-one incident regardless of its rating.

Dependencies are scanned on every build. A build that introduces a known critical vulnerability
fails and cannot be deployed. Security researchers may report issues to the disclosure address
and will receive an acknowledgement within two business days.
